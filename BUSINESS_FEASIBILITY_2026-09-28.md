# Business Feasibility Audit — 2026-09-28

## Gate
First 6 months: zero-cost/free-first. Paid subscriptions only after revenue or a verified production bottleneck.

## Product thesis
AI COO control center: Command → Classify → Policy Check → Approval → Execute → Verify Evidence → Record → Report.

## Market evidence
McKinsey's 2025 global survey reported 88% of respondents' organizations regularly using AI in at least one business function; 62% were at least experimenting with AI agents, while most organizations remained in experimentation/pilot stages. Source: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-2025
Grand View Research estimates the AI automation market at $169.5B in 2026, with a projected 31.4% CAGR through 2033. Source: https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-automation-market-report

## Customer / GTM
Initial ICP: founder-led SMEs and operators who need controlled execution across business tools. Zero-cost GTM: direct founder outreach, public demos, open-source proof, and a free audit/command demo.

## Monetization hypothesis
Free control center/audit → paid managed automation or team plan. Payment is unproven until a real customer converts.

## Economics / timing
Existing deployment can be kept free-first; model/API/tool usage must be measured before any paid scale. Break-even depends on support and automation volume. First revenue milestone: paid pilot using the existing control plane.

## Decision gate
BUILD/VALIDATE: high relevance to current AI adoption, but commercial proof is required.

## Builder contract
Preserve Auth, Policy Gate, Approval, Duplicate Guard, Verification/Evidence and fail-closed behavior. Never simulate execution receipts.
