# ENJY AI COO — Go-To-Market Launch Kit

## Positioning
**AI Operations systems that turn business requests into controlled, observable, verifiable execution.**

### Core offer
Build and deploy governed AI operators for businesses that need more than a chatbot:
- lead qualification and CRM automation
- booking, reminders, and follow-up
- internal AI operations command centers
- API/webhook integrations
- human approval for risky actions
- execution evidence and audit trails

## Buyer profiles
1. Founder / owner of a service business
2. Operations manager with repetitive manual workflows
3. Agency that needs a white-label AI operations layer
4. Team already using CRM + messaging + automation but lacking reliability

## Content pillars
1. **Build in public:** real architecture, tests, failure fixes, verification.
2. **Before vs after:** manual workflow → governed AI operator.
3. **Automation teardown:** show one business process and the exact control points.
4. **Proof:** short demos, screenshots, CI/deployment evidence, case studies.
5. **Founder POV:** why AI agents fail in production and how to govern them.

## 30-day organic launch cadence
- 3 short videos/week
- 2 educational text posts/week
- 1 technical case study/week
- 1 direct offer/week
- Daily comments on 10 relevant founder/ops/automation posts

## Conversion CTA
**“Send me one repetitive business workflow. I’ll map the automation, the failure points, and the fastest safe implementation path.”**

## Proof rules
Never claim revenue, customer count, uptime, production scale, or provider execution unless independently verified. Demo adapters must remain labelled as demos.
